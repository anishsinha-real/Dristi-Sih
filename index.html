❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What is DRISHTI?
DRISHTI is a landslide-awareness and decision-support platform combining map intelligence, risk information, alerts and citizen participation.
Does DRISHTI replace emergency authorities?
No. Always follow authorized emergency agencies and official emergency instructions.
Does SOS automatically contact police?
The current frontend prepares location-based sharing and SMS handoff. Automatic dispatch requires an authorized backend integration.
Can I submit a citizen report?
Yes. Use the Citizen Report section and provide accurate information.
