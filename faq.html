Frequently Asked Questions

DRISHTI FAQ

Is DRISHTI an official emergency authority?

DRISHTI is a decision-support and awareness platform. Follow official authorities during emergencies.

Does SOS automatically dispatch police?

No. The current browser version prepares location-aware sharing and SMS actions; real dispatch requires integrated emergency services.

Where do community reports come from?

They are citizen observations stored locally until backend synchronization is enabled.
